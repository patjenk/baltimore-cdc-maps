Baltimore CDC Areas

Service areas of community development corporations, Main Street programs, land trusts, and other nonprofit developers, plus the Healthy Neighborhoods eligible neighborhoods.

86 organizations researched · 74 mapped · 79 Healthy Neighborhoods neighborhoods

Areas come from state award lists, each organization's website, and city, state, and foundation sources, linked in each map. Dashed outlines mark approximate placements, such as a corridor or an area with no city boundary of its own. Neighborhood boundaries are the Baltimore City Department of Planning's Neighborhood Statistical Areas (March 2023).

An organization's service area can change faster than its website. Check with the organization before relying on a boundary.
